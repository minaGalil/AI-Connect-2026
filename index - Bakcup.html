Governance

The four gates before production

1. Value gate

What decision improves? What cost, risk, or delay is reduced?

2. Data gate

Is the data accurate, governed, and authorized for this use?

3. Risk gate

What can go wrong? What approval is required?

4. Adoption gate

Who will use it, trust it, and own the outcome?

These are the four questions a project must answer before it reaches production. Consider inviting the room to silently apply gate 1 to their own slowest decision — it sets up the Q&A discussion prompts slide later.
